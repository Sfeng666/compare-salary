THE CALCULATION

Local expenses. Dollars left over.

We subtract a local household budget from take-home pay, then find the salary in your comparison city that leaves the same amount. Rent, food, transport, health care and other necessities vary by location.

The remaining dollars keep their face value for savings, travel and purchases whose prices are similar across cities. This is one household-budget model, not a universal measure of living standards.

Calculations run in your browser. Published location data and tax tables are reused without an AI call. County and state approximations are labeled. You can use a local tax engine for a more detailed estimate.
